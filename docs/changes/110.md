- **ADD:** Quasi-vertical profiles (Ryzhkov et al. 2016): ``radarx.retrieve.qvp`` / ``dtree.radarx.qvp()`` averages a high-elevation sweep over azimuth (rhohv > 0.6, Z > -10 dBZ, at least 30 valid gates, dB quantities in linear units) at the beam height, ``qvp_timeseries`` builds time-height QVPs from many volumes, and ``melting_layer`` finds the melting-layer top and bottom from the co-located rhohv minimum and ZDR/Z maxima (after Giangrande et al. 2008), with a time-consistency check and a quality flag. A multithreaded C++ kernel reduces a day of volumes in about 0.1 s, with an identical NumPy fallback. ({pull}`110`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
