- **ADD:** `match_dpr` matches the volumes of a ground radar and the GPM DPR with the parallax of the DPR ray after Schwaller and Morris (2011), `match_imerg` averages a radar rain rate onto the grid and steps of IMERG, `match_statistics` gives the bias, error, correlation and detection scores of matched fields, and `dpr_dsd` and `dpr_parallax` are the helpers for the DPR (Dm and 10 log10 Nw with mu = 3, the convention of the radarx DSD retrieval). The matching has a compiled kernel with a NumPy fallback. GMI matching and the GOES overlays are not part of this change.
