- **ADD:** Probabilistic nowcasting on top of `advect`: `nowcast` makes Lagrangian persistence, S-PROG and STEPS-type ensemble forecasts of gridded reflectivity (Seed 2003; Bowler et al. 2006), `exceedance_probability` turns an ensemble into probabilities and `nowcast_model` runs an ONNX model from `radarx.ml` on the last frames. `optical_flow` adds Lucas-Kanade and DARTS-type motion estimates that `advect` and `nowcast` accept. `fss`, `crps`, `categorical_scores`, `brier_score`, `reliability` and `rank_histogram` score the forecasts (Roberts and Lean 2008; Hersbach 2000). The cascade, the verification and the optical flow run in multithreaded C++ kernels with NumPy equivalents; on ten forecast starts of the KGWX squall line the ensemble has a lower CRPS (4.4 against 5.1 dBZ) and Brier score (0.117 against 0.146) than pysteps with its defaults and the same motion.
