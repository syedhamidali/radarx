- **ADD:** Multi-radar gridding and network calibration: ``radarx.grid.grid_radars`` cone-grids several radars onto one shared grid (common origin and projection, heights above sea level, optional advection to a common time), keeping each radar's fields and the beam azimuth, elevation and slant range from every radar to every cell for multi-Doppler retrievals; ``network_bias`` estimates relative ZH/ZDR biases from the overlap regions and reconciles them by weighted least squares against a reference radar; ``merge_radars`` builds a merged field with range, beam-elevation and time weights (Zhang et al. 2005; Lakshmanan et al. 2006). Geodesics, cone gridding, geometry and merging run in multithreaded C++ kernels with NumPy fallbacks; four NEXRAD radars on a 4.7 M-cell grid take 0.7 s.
