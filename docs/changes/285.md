- **MNT:** One name per concept in the arguments of every function. The first argument is `obj` (was `ds`, `dtree`, `radar`, `volume` or `grid`), reflectivity is `dbz` (was `dbzh` or `reflectivity`), the radial velocity is `field` (was `velocity`, `vel` or `vrad`), thresholds are `min_<quantity>` (`min_dbz`, `min_rhohv`, `min_snr`, `min_zdr`, `min_kdp`; was `dbz_min`, `rhohv_min`, `rho_min`, `snr_min`, `zdr_min`, `kdp_min`, `min_reflectivity`), the beam width is `beamwidth` (was `beam_width`), `superobs` takes `rhohv` (was `rho`), and the fields to process are `data_vars` in `create_cappi`, `apply_mask`, `write_odim`, `radar_at_location` and `match_radar` (was `fields`). `n_threads` comes right before `engine` in every signature that has both (51 public functions had `engine` first), and both are keyword-only everywhere; in `grid_radar` the arguments after `method` (`n_threads`, `fill_depth`, `fill_velocity`, `k_e`) are keyword-only now, and `hitschfeld_bordan_correction` takes `engine` instead of `use_compiled`. The old names work for one release: 102 aliases in 74 functions (`radarx._compat.renamed_arguments`) give a `DeprecationWarning` that names the old and the new argument, and positional use of the arguments that became keyword-only warns too. The `.radarx` accessor methods take the signature of the function they call (names, defaults, keyword-only markers) instead of `**kwargs`, so `help` and tab completion list the arguments. The conventions are listed in the user guide. ({issue}`267`, {pull}`285`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **MNT:** One dtype policy and UDUNITS unit strings for the results. A floating point field that a function returns is `float32` whatever the precision of the input; `estimate_kdp`, `create_cappi`, `dsd`, `dsd_bayesian`, `correct_attenuation`, `vad_profile` and `melting_layer` returned `float64` before. The kernels and the NumPy fallbacks compute in `float64` and round once at the end, so the two engines agree to `float32` rounding (the engine comparison tests of `estimate_kdp` use `rtol=2e-7`). Coordinates, the tables of `rotation_couplets`, the storm motion of `estimate_motion` and corrected copies of an input field (`dealias_velocity`, `apply_mask`) keep their precision. The `units` attributes are in the UDUNITS style: `degree km-1` and `dB km-1` (were `degrees/km` and `dB/km`), `degree`, `m`, `1`, `%`, `m s-1` and `m-3 mm-1` (were `degrees`, `meters`, `unitless`, `percent`, `meters per second` and `m-3 mm-(1+mu)`); the IMD reader keeps the spellings of the xradar readers. A test runs the main functions on a synthetic volume with `float32` and `float64` fields and checks the dtype and the units grammar of every output variable, and scans the source for the old spellings. - **ADD:** `ensemble_retrieval` (also `.radarx.ensemble_retrieval`) propagates random errors of the radar measurements, the calibration and the DSD prior through `estimate_kdp`, `correct_attenuation`, `rain_rate_z`, `rain_rate_kdp`, `rain_rate_z_zdr`, `rain_rate_a`, `dsd`, `dsd_bayesian`, `hid` or any function by Monte Carlo and returns the mean, median, standard deviation and percentiles of every product (`KDP_mean`, `KDP_median`, `KDP_std`, `KDP_p05`, `KDP_p95`, class codes as `HID_mode` and `HID_agreement`, `X_total_std` for the Bayesian retrieval, all members as `X_members` on a `member` dimension with `keep_members=True`). `perturb_radar` makes the members: one calibration offset of `DBZH` (1 dB) and `ZDR` (0.1 dB) per member, in antithetic pairs, and the measurement noise of every gate from the standard deviations of Melnikov and Zrnic (2004) for `ZDR`, `PHIDP`, `RHOHV`, the reflectivity and the pulse-pair velocity from the number of pulses, the SNR, `RHOHV` and the spectrum width (`measurement_error` returns them; the formulas agree with Monte Carlo simulations of the estimators within 2 %); the DSD prior part uses the scatter of the rain rate relations and of A/KDP that `relation_scatter` computes from the generic prior, in the manner of the weighting of Ryzhkov et al. (2014). `ensemble_statistics` reduces any array with a `member` dimension. The random numbers are counter based (Philox-4x64-10, Salmon et al. 2011) in a compiled kernel, with a NumPy twin that reads the same 64-bit words from `numpy.random.Philox`, so the members do not depend on the number of threads (the Gaussian numbers agree between the engines to rounding, not bit for bit); the statistics run in a threaded kernel too. On a synthetic sweep with known truth the ratio of the RMS error to the RMS spread is 0.96 to 1.01 for the rain rate from Z, the DSD retrievals and the attenuation correction, and 0.5 to 0.6 for KDP, whose filter makes the spread larger than the error. The new notebook `Ensemble_Uncertainty` shows the error model, the test against a known truth and maps of the spread for KLBB and CSAPR2, and the advanced workflow has a section 16. ({issue}`213`, {pull}`285`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
