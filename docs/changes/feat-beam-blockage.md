- **ADD:** `beam_blockage` computes the partial beam blockage (`PBB`, the part of the beam power below the terrain, Bech et al. 2003, with a Gaussian beam pattern as in Lang et al. 2009 or the uniform circle of the paper), the cumulative beam blockage (`CBB`, the maximum of `PBB` along the ray), the terrain height under the gate and a terrain-clutter flag for every gate of a sweep or volume from a digital elevation model given as a `DataArray` (latitude and longitude, or projected `x` and `y` with a `crs`; radarx does not download terrain). The beam follows the 4/3 Earth radius model with a `k_e` argument and the site altitude in the Earth radius, the beam width comes from the sweep metadata or an argument, and a compiled multithreaded kernel with a NumPy fallback does the work; DataArray, Dataset and DataTree input, lazy dask arrays, the `.radarx.beam_blockage` accessor method and the new notebook `Beam_Blockage`. The result is not applied to any field: use `CBB` as a mask or quality field. ({issue}`207`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
