- **ADD:** Bayesian drop size distribution retrieval with per-gate uncertainty (``radarx.retrieve.dsd_bayesian``, ``ds.radarx.dsd_bayesian()``, also on DataTree volumes): the posterior of a normalized gamma DSD (log10 Nw, Dm, mu) given Z_H, Z_DR and, where available, K_DP and the specific attenuation, with T-matrix forward operators from radarx's scattering tables, a measurement-error model (noise, calibration bias, K_DP and A_H errors) and priors from disdrometers (``dsd_prior``: a generic prior after Bringi et al. 2003 and Cao et al. 2008, one learned from trajectory-matched PERiLS 2022 PIPS spectra, or one learned from any fitted spectra). Returns posterior means, standard deviations, credible intervals and the MAP of Nw, Dm, mu, rain rate and water content, with a misfit and the evidence that flag non-rain. A multithreaded C++ kernel evaluates the posterior on a (Dm, mu) grid with Laplace integration in Nw, with an identical NumPy fallback. Analysis code, trajectory matching and the PERiLS validation are in ``ml/models/bayesian_dsd``. ({pull}`154`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
