- **ADD:** `dual_doppler_analytic` solves the horizontal wind of every cell from the radial velocities of two or more radars by weighted least squares (the 2 x 2 system of the classical dual-Doppler analysis), returns the Gauss-Markov standard deviation of the error of `u` and `v` (for two radars and equal errors `sigma`, `sqrt((cos^2 phi_1 + cos^2 phi_2) / (cos^2 phi_1 cos^2 phi_2)) sigma / sin(beta)` for the combined horizontal error, which is derived in the docstring and checked in the tests) and integrates the anelastic continuity equation upward, downward or upward with the O'Brien adjustment. It is for quick checks and for validating `multi_doppler`; compiled multithreaded kernels with NumPy fallbacks, `.radarx.dual_doppler_analytic` and a section of the notebook `Multi_Doppler`. On the Beltrami test flow with 0.5 m/s noise (beams crossing at more than 30 degrees) the rms errors of `u`, `v` and `w` are 0.55, 0.88 and 0.58 m/s for the analytic solver with the O'Brien adjustment and 0.19, 0.34 and 0.23 m/s for `multi_doppler` with the same boundary condition; without noise the analytic `u` is exact and its `v` and `w` carry 0.6 and 0.3 m/s from the coupled horizontal-wind and continuity iteration, and upward integration without the adjustment is worse (`v` 5.5 m/s after three passes). Where the lowest levels are not seen by two radars (KGWX and KBMX below 3 km) the integrals are NaN. ({issue}`237`)
- **ADD:** `multi_doppler(w_boundary=("bottom", "obrien"))` adjusts the retrieved vertical velocity so that it is zero at the highest level with the O'Brien (1970) correction of the mass flux (`obrien_error="linear"`, default, or `"constant"`). The implementation follows the open ARPS ADAS code and the GEMPAK documentation of the correction; the paper could not be read and the docstring says so. ({issue}`237`)
- **ADD:** `multi_doppler(obs_error=...)` weights the radial velocities by the inverse square of their error (one number, one per radar, or an array or `DataArray`; NaN removes the observation) and `crossing_angle_weight=True` multiplies the weights by the squared sine of the beam crossing angle. `multi_doppler(error=True)` returns `u_error`, `v_error` and `w_error`, the standard deviation of the retrieval error from the covariance of the cost function (the inverse of half its Hessian), estimated from `error_samples` conjugate-gradient draws. On the Beltrami flow with 0.5 m/s noise the estimate is 1.04 times (`w`), 1.27 times (`v`) and 1.44 times (`u`) the rms error. All options are off by default; the results and the output of `multi_doppler` without them are unchanged. A formula for the error of the radial velocity from the signal to noise ratio is not included (the pulse-pair variance of Zrnic 1977 and Doviak and Zrnic 1993 could not be read). A terrain-following lower boundary from a digital elevation model and the mixed-order interpolation of Collis et al. (2010) are not implemented. ({issue}`237`)
