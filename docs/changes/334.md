- **ADD:** `melting_layer_gradient` finds the melting layer top and bottom of a vertical profile (QVP, range-defined QVP, columnar profile or RHI profile) from the gradients of Z, ZDR and rhohv and of their products with 1 - rhohv, following Allabakash et al. (2019), and `melting_layer_rhi` builds the profile of an RHI scan with the settings of the paper first. The top rule of the paper searches the whole profile, which puts the top 1 to 3 km too high on the QVPs tried here; `top_window` (off by default, radarx's addition) searches only near the layer. A compiled multithreaded kernel with a NumPy fallback does the detection, `.radarx.melting_layer_gradient` and `.radarx.melting_layer_rhi` are the accessor methods, and `melting_layer`, `qvp` and `melting_layer_ppi` are unchanged. The beam-broadening tables are not implemented. ({issue}`256`, {pull}`334`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
