- **FIX:** `dealias_velocity(method="region")` can rewrite the core of a velocity couplet that lies inside the Nyquist interval. A couplet with peaks of about 0.95 Vn that is joined by small steps to a region that really is folded is shifted with that region, because most border pairs of the joined region are fold lines, and its core goes to speeds of 2 to 3 Vn. On the 0.5° cut of KBMX (27 April 2011, Nyquist velocity 29.97 m/s) the peaks inside the Nyquist interval, -30 and +30 m/s, became -88 and +26 m/s (velocity difference 114 m/s). The new argument `max_speed` (m/s, default None) moves a small cluster of gates (at most 100 connected gates) that the folds put beyond that speed back, one fold at a time, while that lowers the velocity differences to the neighbouring gates that are folded less; a gate beside a less folded gate that it matches only with its fold keeps it, so the plateau of a real fold stays unfolded. With `max_speed=36` the KBMX peaks are -36 and +26 m/s (velocity difference 62 m/s), with 45 m/s -44 and +26 m/s (70 m/s), and the unfolded plateau next to the couplet (-31 to -34 m/s) is kept; UNRAVEL gives -37 and +30 m/s. The default does not change: the sweep alone cannot tell the shifted core from a strong wind (both readings are smooth, and the shifted one has fewer jumps across its borders), the limit changes 0.1 % of the gates of the whole volume (1 782 of 1.4 million at 36 m/s) and raises the number of neighbouring pairs that differ by more than Vn from 6 476 to 7 499, so it is the choice of the user. A compiled kernel and a NumPy implementation give identical folds. A synthetic outbound pocket beside a folded patch reproduces the failure in the tests. ({issue}`287`, {pull}`293`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **FIX:** The docstring of `dealias_velocity` states one rule for gates at the Nyquist velocity and the gates that UNRAVEL leaves without a value. A velocity up to 1 % beyond the Nyquist velocity is data in both methods (velocities are coded in steps of 0.5 m/s, so the largest code of a radar with a Nyquist velocity of 29.97 m/s is 30.0 m/s) and a larger magnitude is a flag value, which becomes NaN. `method="unravel"` also returns NaN where its first step takes a gate for noise: the velocity is more than two median absolute deviations from the median of the 10 gates around it along the ray and no unfolding brings it within 0.3 Vn of the window mean. That removed 1 702 of 173 579 gates of the KBMX 0.5° cut (1.0 %), including all 48 dropped gates of the couplet box, and 12 of the 1 740 gates at 30.0 m/s. The region method gives a value to every gate with data. Tests cover both rules. ({issue}`288`, {pull}`293`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
