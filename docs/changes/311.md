- **ADD:** `zdr_arc` finds the ZDR arc, the storm objects and the KDP foot of the supercells of a grid, and gives the arc area, the mean and the top-10 ZDR of the arc, and the length and angle (to the storm motion) of the KDP-ZDR separation vector, after Wilson and Van Den Broeke (2021). The random forest of the paper that removes spurious candidates cannot be rebuilt from the paper, so `classifier` takes a model or a function of the features of Table 1. No tracking between scans.
