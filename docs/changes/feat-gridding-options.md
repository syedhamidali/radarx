- **FIX:** The grids of `grid_cones`, `grid_radars` and the Cartesian `create_cappi` are georeferenced as CF when they are built, with one shared builder (`radarx._gridcf`) that `write_netcdf_cf` also uses. `lat` and `lon` are now 2-D on `(y, x)` computed from the grid's azimuthal equidistant CRS (the 1-D `lat(y)` and `lon(x)` of `grid_cones` were 633 m off in the corner of a grid of 120 km width and 9 km off at 230 km range; the new ones agree with the geodesic to 1 cm), every field with `x` and `y` has `grid_mapping = "crs_wkt"`, and `x`, `y`, `z` and `time` have `standard_name`, `units`, `axis` and `positive`; cf_xarray and rioxarray find the CRS. `create_cappi` had no CRS; it now has the one centred on the radar, which is what xradar's `x` and `y` are. Code that used `grid.lat` as a vector along `y` must use `grid.lat.sel(x=0)` or the 2-D arrays. Files written by `write_netcdf_cf` do not change. ({issue}`265`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
