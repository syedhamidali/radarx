- **ADD:** `tc_center`, `gbvtd`, `tc_decompose`, `synthetic_vortex` and `vortex_profile` retrieve the winds of a tropical cyclone from one Doppler radar. `gbvtd` fits Fourier series to the Doppler velocity on rings around a centre (the ground-based velocity track display, Lee et al. 1999) and returns the axisymmetric tangential and radial wind, the wavenumber-1 and 2 tangential wind (amplitude and azimuth), the along-beam mean wind, the radius of maximum wind and the fit diagnostics, with the closure that the asymmetric radial wind is zero, the cross-beam mean wind removed when a mean wind or storm motion is given, and the Fourier order reduced for wide data gaps. `tc_center` finds the centre and the radius of maximum wind as the centre that maximises the axisymmetric tangential wind of GBVTD, with a simplex search from a 4 by 4 grid of starting points per ring radius and the consensus and NORM score of Bell and Lee (2012) for one volume, from a first guess given as metres or latitude and longitude or from the velocity dipole. `tc_decompose` splits the retrieval into the symmetric part and the wavenumber-1 and 2 parts on a polar grid. `synthetic_vortex` forward-models a modified Rankine-combined vortex (Chen et al. 2013) with a mean wind, asymmetries, noise, beam height and data gaps to a Doppler sweep. A compiled multithreaded kernel with a NumPy fallback does the ring fits and searches; DataArray, Dataset and DataTree input, lazy dask arrays, the `.radarx.tc_center` and `.radarx.gbvtd` accessor methods and the new notebook `Tropical_Cyclone_Winds`. The text of Lee et al. (1999) and of Lee and Marks (2000) could not be read: the equations come from Liou et al. (2005) and Chen et al. (2013) and the code is not checked against the 1999 and 2000 papers; the temporal steps of Bell and Lee, the modified GBVTD, two-radar GBVTD and eyewall and rainband diagnostics are not included. On the Tauktae volume of the IMD Goa radar the cyclone is 310 km away and no ring has enough velocity data, so nothing is retrieved.
