- **ADD:** `ensemble_retrieval` (also `.radarx.ensemble_retrieval`) propagates random errors of the radar measurements, the calibration and the DSD prior through `estimate_kdp`, `correct_attenuation`, `rain_rate_z`, `rain_rate_kdp`, `rain_rate_z_zdr`, `rain_rate_a`, `dsd`, `dsd_bayesian`, `hid` or any function by Monte Carlo and returns the mean, median, standard deviation and percentiles of every product (`KDP_mean`, `KDP_median`, `KDP_std`, `KDP_p05`, `KDP_p95`, class codes as `HID_mode` and `HID_agreement`, `X_total_std` for the Bayesian retrieval, all members as `X_members` on a `member` dimension with `keep_members=True`). `perturb_radar` makes the members: one calibration offset of `DBZH` (1 dB) and `ZDR` (0.1 dB) per member, in antithetic pairs, and the measurement noise of every gate from the standard deviations of Melnikov and Zrnic (2004) for `ZDR`, `PHIDP`, `RHOHV`, the reflectivity and the pulse-pair velocity from the number of pulses, the SNR, `RHOHV` and the spectrum width (`measurement_error` returns them; the formulas agree with Monte Carlo simulations of the estimators within 2 %); the DSD prior part uses the scatter of the rain rate relations and of A/KDP that `relation_scatter` computes from the generic prior, in the manner of the weighting of Ryzhkov et al. (2014). `ensemble_statistics` reduces any array with a `member` dimension. The random numbers are counter based (Philox-4x64-10, Salmon et al. 2011) in a compiled kernel, with a NumPy twin that reads the same 64-bit words from `numpy.random.Philox`, so the members do not depend on the number of threads (the Gaussian numbers agree between the engines to rounding, not bit for bit); the statistics run in a threaded kernel too. On a synthetic sweep with known truth the ratio of the RMS error to the RMS spread is 0.96 to 1.01 for the rain rate from Z, the DSD retrievals and the attenuation correction, and 0.5 to 0.6 for KDP, whose filter makes the spread larger than the error. The new notebook `Ensemble_Uncertainty` shows the error model, the test against a known truth and maps of the spread for KLBB and CSAPR2, and the advanced workflow has a section 16.
