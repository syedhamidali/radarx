- **ADD:** Variational multi-Doppler 3D wind retrieval (``radarx.retrieve.multi_doppler``, ``grids.radarx.multi_doppler()``) after Gao et al. (1999): radial velocities of any number of radars with fall-speed correction (Atlas et al. 1973), anelastic mass continuity, smoothness, a sounding or ERA5 background and an optional vertical vorticity constraint (Shapiro et al. 2009; Potvin et al. 2012). A multithreaded C++ kernel computes the cost and its exact gradient in one fused pass (NumPy fallback); the quadratic cost is minimised by DCT-preconditioned conjugate gradients from coarse to fine grids (L-BFGS-B with the vorticity term), with w boundary conditions as a change of variables. ``multi_doppler_input`` grids and advects several volumes onto one grid with the beam geometry (``radar_geometry``). On an analytic flow sampled by two radars it is 6 times faster and 2-3 times more accurate than PyDDA. ({pull}`129`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
