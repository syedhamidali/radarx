- **ADD:** `hail_products`, `shi`, `mesh`, `posh` and `hail_kinetic_energy` compute the severe hail index, the maximum expected hail size, the probability of severe hail and the hail kinetic energy flux of Witt et al. (1998) for every (azimuth, ground range) column of a polar volume, grid or quasi-vertical profile, from the 0 and -20 degC heights given as numbers, as 2-D fields or taken from a sounding or ERA5 profile, with a compiled multithreaded kernel and a NumPy fallback; the new notebook `Hail_Products` checks them against closed-form columns and the worked example of the paper and shows them on KGWX data. The probability of hail (POH) and a comparison with MRMS MESH are not included. ({issue}`208`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
