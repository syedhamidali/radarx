- **FIX:** `dealias_velocity(method="region")` can shift the core of a velocity couplet by one fold together with a folded region next to it (#287). The sweep alone cannot tell this from a strong wind, so the values stay as they are, and the result is checked: a block of at most 200 connected gates shifted by one fold that holds 10 or more gates beyond 2 Vn (and 50 m/s) raises a `UserWarning` with the number of pockets, gates and the largest speed, and points to `max_speed`. On the 0.5° cut of KBMX (27 April 2011) it names one pocket of 13 gates with speeds up to 88 m/s, the core of the couplet; `max_speed=36` gives -36 and +26 m/s there. ({issue}`287`, {pull}`325`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
