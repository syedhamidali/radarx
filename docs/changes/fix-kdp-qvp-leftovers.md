- **FIX:** `qvp(elevation_correction=True)` and `qvp_timeseries` also refer ZDR to 0 degrees elevation, with the inverse of the relation `zdr_offset` applies (Rayleigh scattering by spheroids with a vertical axis and no canting, derived in radarx and not checked against a paper); KDP was already divided by `cos(elevation)**2`. The default (`False`) is unchanged. ({issue}`247`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **ENH:** `estimate_kdp(method="hubbert")` returns the backscatter phase `PHIDP_BACKSCATTER` (measured minus processed phase, a lower bound of delta), every method returns `KDP_UNCERTAINTY` (the phase noise of the ray passed through the filter and the least-squares slope), and `PHIDP_processed` records `phase_noise`, `filter_window` and `kdp_reference`. The band factors 1, 2 and 3 of `band` are radarx choices, not from a paper, and are now the table `BAND_FACTORS` that the new `band_factors` argument updates. ({issue}`249`, {issue}`254`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **FIX:** The default `filter_window=None` of `estimate_kdp(method="hubbert")` takes, for each sweep, the narrowest window (at most the former 2 km) for which the noise of KDP stays below `noise_fraction` (0.04) of the largest KDP of the sweep, with the phase noise estimated from the second differences of the phase. S and C-band cells keep the 2 km window and give the same numbers; the peak of a narrow X-band cell (true 11.99 degrees/km) is 11.75 and no longer 9.86, the RMSE falls from 0.86 to 0.58 degrees/km. `filter_window=2.0` restores the former behaviour. Wang and Chandrasekar (2009) is not implemented because neither the paper nor an open description of it could be read. ({issue}`273`, {issue}`254`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
