- **FIX:** `doppler_frequency_shift(relativistic=True)` returns the two-way relativistic shift `f ((1+b)/(1-b) - 1)` (373.6 Hz for 5.6 GHz and 10 m/s) and no longer the one-way shift, which was half the radar value; it now equals the `exact` result. (issue {issue}`167`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **FIX:** `dual_prf_velocity` returns the positive magnitude `lambda / (4 |1/prf1 - 1/prf2|)` for either order of the two PRFs; it was negative for `prf1 > prf2` (-75 m/s for `(0.1, 1000, 750)`). (issue {issue}`168`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **FIX:** `scattering.backscatter_cross_section` and `variables.reflectivity_factor` use the `dielectric` argument as `|K|^2`, as its default 0.93 is, and no longer square it: the cross section was 0.30 dB low and Z 7.5 percent too large with the default. The `Z` of `reflectivity_factor` is in m^3 for the SI `radar_const` (multiply by 1e18 for mm^6 m^-3), now stated in the docstring. The Rayleigh absorption of `scattering` and `attenuation` uses `|Im(K)|`, so `n - jk` and `n + jk` give the same value (`scattering` returned 0 and `attenuation` a negative value for the other sign). (issue {issue}`194`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **FIX:** `variables.linear_depolarization_ratio(z_co, z_cross)` documents its arguments as the co-polar and the cross-polar power (`10 log10(z_cross / z_co)`, LDR_vh of Bringi and Chandrasekar 2001, Eq. 3.169); they were called `z_h` and `z_v`, for which the result is `-ZDR`, and the keyword names change while positional calls give the same numbers. `circular_depolarization_ratio` keeps its numbers and its docstring now says that `z_parallel` is the channel received in the transmit sense (`S_RR`) and `z_orthogonal` the opposite sense (`S_LR`). (issue {issue}`195`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **FIX:** `beam.compute_beamwidth` returns `1.27 lambda / D`, the 3 dB beamwidth of a tapered paraboloid (Doviak and Zrnic 1993, Eq. 3.2b), and no longer `1.22 lambda / D` (first null), so every result is 4 percent larger; for the WSR-88D antenna the error against the published 0.96 and 0.88 deg drops from -5.3 and -7.1 percent to -1.5 and -3.2 percent. The new `factor` argument keeps 1.22 or the factor of a known antenna available. (issue {issue}`196`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **FIX:** The names defined in two `radarx.fundamentals` modules (`absorption_coefficient`, `scattering_coefficient` and `extinction_coefficient` of `attenuation` and `scattering`, and `doppler_frequency_shift` of `doppler` and `principles`) are no longer exported by `radarx.fundamentals`, where the later module silently won; use the module path, for example `scattering.absorption_coefficient`. The plain package-level names still return the function they used to return, with a `DeprecationWarning`, and are removed after the next release. (issue {issue}`196`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **SCI:** The new `reflectivity.hitschfeld_bordan_correction` corrects the reflectivity for attenuation with the path-integrated Hitschfeld-Bordan solution for `k = a Z^b` (compiled multithreaded kernel with a NumPy fallback, lazy for dask-backed DataArrays, correction capped at `max_correction_db`, 10 dB by default). `reflectivity.dbz_attenuation_correction`, which added a correction from the local value only, is deprecated and warns. (issue {issue}`234`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **MNT:** `geometry.beam_center_height`, `ground_range` and `beam_height_at_ground_range` put the site altitude in the Earth radius like the gridding code and xradar (`sqrt(r^2 + (R + alt)^2 + 2 r (R + alt) sin(e)) - R`); the heights change by less than 1 m up to 200 km range and 2 km altitude. `ground_range` has a new `radar_height` argument, passed by `radarx.retrieve.vil` and `radar_at_location`. (issue {issue}`235`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **FIX:** The converters of `radarx.fundamentals.common` (`dbz_to_z`, `z_to_dbz`, `km_to_m`, `m_to_km`, `kts_to_mps`, `mps_to_kts` and their aliases) and `reflectivity.z_to_r_marshall_palmer` and `z_to_r_custom` keep the dims, coords and name of a DataArray, set `units` and `long_name` for the converted quantity and stay lazy for dask input; they returned bare ndarrays and computed dask arrays. Scalars and lists still give NumPy results. (issue {issue}`261`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
