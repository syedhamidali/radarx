- **MNT:** One name per concept in the arguments of every function. The first argument is `obj` (was `ds`, `dtree`, `radar`, `volume` or `grid`), reflectivity is `dbz` (was `dbzh` or `reflectivity`), the radial velocity is `field` (was `velocity`, `vel` or `vrad`), thresholds are `min_<quantity>` (`min_dbz`, `min_rhohv`, `min_snr`, `min_zdr`, `min_kdp`; was `dbz_min`, `rhohv_min`, `rho_min`, `snr_min`, `zdr_min`, `kdp_min`, `min_reflectivity`), the beam width is `beamwidth` (was `beam_width`), `superobs` takes `rhohv` (was `rho`), and the fields to process are `data_vars` in `create_cappi`, `apply_mask`, `write_odim`, `radar_at_location` and `match_radar` (was `fields`). `n_threads` comes right before `engine` in every signature that has both (51 public functions had `engine` first), and both are keyword-only everywhere; in `grid_radar` the arguments after `method` (`n_threads`, `fill_depth`, `fill_velocity`, `k_e`) are keyword-only now, and `hitschfeld_bordan_correction` takes `engine` instead of `use_compiled`. The old names work for one release: 102 aliases in 74 functions (`radarx._compat.renamed_arguments`) give a `DeprecationWarning` that names the old and the new argument, and positional use of the arguments that became keyword-only warns too. The `.radarx` accessor methods take the signature of the function they call (names, defaults, keyword-only markers) instead of `**kwargs`, so `help` and tab completion list the arguments. The conventions are listed in the user guide. ({issue}`267`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
- **MNT:** One dtype policy and UDUNITS unit strings for the results. A floating point field that a function returns is `float32` whatever the precision of the input; `estimate_kdp`, `create_cappi`, `dsd`, `dsd_bayesian`, `correct_attenuation`, `vad_profile` and `melting_layer` returned `float64` before. The kernels and the NumPy fallbacks compute in `float64` and round once at the end, so the two engines agree to `float32` rounding (the engine comparison tests of `estimate_kdp` use `rtol=2e-7`). Coordinates, the tables of `rotation_couplets`, the storm motion of `estimate_motion` and corrected copies of an input field (`dealias_velocity`, `apply_mask`) keep their precision. The `units` attributes are in the UDUNITS style: `degree km-1` and `dB km-1` (were `degrees/km` and `dB/km`), `degree`, `m`, `1`, `%`, `m s-1` and `m-3 mm-1` (were `degrees`, `meters`, `unitless`, `percent`, `meters per second` and `m-3 mm-(1+mu)`); the IMD reader keeps the spellings of the xradar readers. A test runs the main functions on a synthetic volume with `float32` and `float64` fields and checks the dtype and the units grammar of every output variable, and scans the source for the old spellings. ({issue}`270`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
