- **ADD:** `vvp_winds` fits the volume velocity processing model (uniform, linear, linear_w and full) and returns winds, gradients, divergence, deformation and standard errors; `tc_ring_stats` gives the azimuthal mean and wavenumbers 1 to 3 of a polar field around a centre; `tc_structure` gives the eyewall, moat and secondary maxima, level radii, vorticity and angular momentum. On Hurricane Harvey (KCRP) the centre is 3.7 km from the best track and the maximum wind 60.5 against 59.2 m/s. The modified and two-radar GBVTD are not included.
