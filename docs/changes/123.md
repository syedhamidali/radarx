- **ADD:** Fast non-meteorological echo filtering (``radarx.retrieve.echo_mask`` / ``apply_mask``, ``ds.radarx.echo_mask()`` / ``dtree.radarx.echo_mask()`` and ``.apply_mask()``): a fuzzy-logic score of rhohv, ZDR, ZDR and PHIDP texture, reflectivity texture and spin change (after Gourley et al. 2007, Krause 2016, Steiner and Smith 2002), averaged over 3 x 3 gates, with a speckle filter, classifies every gate as meteorological, non-meteorological or speckle. NEXRAD no-data codes are recognised and the Doppler cuts of NEXRAD split cuts take the class of the surveillance cut. A multithreaded C++ kernel classifies a 16-million-gate NEXRAD volume in about 0.1 s, with an identical NumPy fallback. On KGWX (30 March 2022) it removes 97 % of the biological echo east of the radar and none of the squall line. New notebook. ({pull}`123`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
