- **ADD:** T-matrix scattering of raindrops in C++: `raindrop_scattering` gives the quantities of the radarx scattering tables for any wavelength, temperature, axis ratio and canting width, `spheroid_scattering` the amplitude and phase matrices of spheroids in any directions and orientations, with `orientation_quadrature`, `water_refractive_index` and `axis_ratio_brandes`. The code is a re-entrant, threaded C++ translation of the FORTRAN code of Mishchenko that `pytmatrix` wraps; it reproduces the 1920 rows of the packaged table to 1e-6, builds the whole table in 2 s, and `ci/build_dsd_tables.py` no longer needs `pytmatrix`. Canting and frequency are now selectable inputs (at S band 20 instead of 7 degrees of canting lowers the ZDR of a 4 mm drop by 0.65 dB, of an 8 mm drop by 1.7 dB, and KDP by 27 %). ({issue}`274`, {pull}`305`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
