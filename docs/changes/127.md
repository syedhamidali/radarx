- **ENH:** ``melting_layer`` places the melting-layer top and bottom where the rhohv signature begins and ends (``boundaries="onset"``, the new default, after Griffin et al. 2020; ``"half_prominence"`` keeps the previous edges) and takes ``environment=``, a sounding or ERA5 profile from ``radarx.io.sounding``, to return the 0 °C and wet-bulb 0 °C heights with the offsets of the layer top and to constrain the search. On the KGWX QVPs of 30-31 March 2022 the top is now within 40 m of the ERA5 wet-bulb 0 °C height instead of 320 m below it. ({pull}`127`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
