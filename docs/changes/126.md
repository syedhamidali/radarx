- **ADD:** Drop size distribution retrieval (``radarx.retrieve.dsd``, ``ds.radarx.dsd()`` / ``dtree.radarx.dsd()``): constrained-gamma (Zhang et al. 2001; Cao et al. 2008) and normalized-gamma (Testud et al. 2001; Bringi et al. 2002) DSDs from ZH and ZDR, with the intercept from KDP where it is large enough, a rain mask and an Nw plausibility screen; outputs N0, Nw, D0, Dm, mu, Lambda, rain rate and LWC for sweeps, volumes, grids or QVPs. Lookup tables come from T-matrix scattering tables for S, C and X band shipped with radarx (``ci/build_dsd_tables.py``). A multithreaded C++ kernel retrieves a NEXRAD volume in about 0.2 s, with an identical NumPy fallback. Disdrometer helpers: ``parsivel_bins``, ``dsd_spectrum``, ``fit_gamma_moments`` and ``radar_from_dsd``. New notebook. ({pull}`126`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
