- **ADD:** `rain_shaft_bulk` (also `.radarx.rain_shaft_bulk` on a Dataset) is the two-moment counterpart of `rain_shaft`: number concentration and rainwater content with the gamma shape parameter, fall speeds, self-collection and breakup relaxing toward 1.1 mm of Seifert (2008, Appendix and Eqs. 19, 20), in a compiled multithreaded kernel with a NumPy twin. The sign of Eq. (A12) is taken as a loss. It conserves the rain rate, agrees with an independent SciPy integration to 2e-6 and reaches a mean volume diameter of 1.10 mm where the bin model with Low and List breakup reaches 1.26 mm. `ducting_layers` (also `.radarx.ducting_layers`) and `modified_refractivity` find the layers of a refractivity profile that trap the beam, with their strength and the elevation below which rays are trapped (Bean and Dutton 1966, Table 1.9, Eqs. 4.17, 4.22, 4.23): the angles of the book for layers of 100 m and 30 m (8.3 and 4.5 mrad) are reproduced, and rays traced with `trace_rays` turn at the height of Snell's law below the angle and leave above it. `hail_fall_speed` and the trajectory model get the hail law of Milbrandt and Yau (2005) with its air density factor; the laws of Heymsfield et al. keep no density correction. The wavelength of the radar in ducting, the hail laws of Knight and Heymsfield (1983) and Heymsfield and Wright (2014), the McFarquhar (2004) fragments and the full Seifert and Beheng scheme are not implemented.
