- **ADD:** `zdr_column` gives the depth of the ZDR column above the freezing level on a grid, `zdr_column_metrics` its maximum depth, area and volume, after the algorithm of Snyder et al. (2015): the vertically continuous depth with ZDR of at least 1 dB from the first level at or above the freezing level, grid points of low correlation coefficient removed. A C++ kernel with a NumPy twin; `min_dbz` (not in the paper) leaves out weak echo, which makes columns of up to 6.5 km over 1383 km2 into 2.25 km over 141 km2 on a squall line of PERiLS IOP2. KDP columns are not included. ({issue}`145`, {pull}`309`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
