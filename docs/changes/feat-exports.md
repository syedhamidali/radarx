- **ADD:** `radarx.io.write_cfradial1`, `write_cfradial2` and `write_odim` write a `DataTree` volume with its derived fields to CfRadial1, CfRadial2 and ODIM_H5 through the xradar writers, `write_zarr` writes any radarx result to Zarr (identical on reading back, dask arrays stay lazy) and `write_netcdf_cf` writes a grid with CF georeferencing (`crs` grid mapping from the `crs_wkt`, `grid_mapping` on every field, `standard_name`, `units` and `axis` on the coordinates, 2-D `lat` and `lon` from the CRS, cell bounds); each has a `.radarx.to_cfradial1`, `to_cfradial2`, `to_odim`, `to_zarr` or `to_netcdf_cf` method. The input is never changed and the arrays are not copied. Attributes that NetCDF cannot store are converted, `_FillValue` is moved to the encoding of float fields and removed from integer fields (the HID class codes), stale encodings are dropped, the provenance (`radarx_method`, `radarx_references`, `radarx_version`, `history`) is merged onto the global attributes of CfRadial1 and CfRadial2 (which have no sweep attributes for it) and written to the `how` groups of ODIM_H5, and the CfRadial1 writer no longer stops when the sweeps hold different fields. What each format cannot hold is in the docstrings and in the new notebook `Exporting_Results`. Metadata and I/O only, so there is no C++ kernel.
