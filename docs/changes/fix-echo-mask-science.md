- **SCI:** `echo_mask(method="krause2016")` implements the MetSignal algorithm of Krause (2016): the memberships of the paper's Fig. 2 for reflectivity, rho_hv, radial velocity and the standard deviations of PhiDP, ZDR and rho_hv over nine range bins, the weights 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 1 of its Table 1, the threshold 0.8 (warm season, default) or 0.7 (cold season, `threshold=0.7`), the rules that abs(ZDR) > 4.5 dB and rho_hv < 0.65 are non-meteorological and the override of gates with at least 11 dBZ at 3 km height in the previous volume (`zh_3km`, a field the caller builds). Compiled multithreaded kernel with a NumPy fallback that gives the same numbers; the new options `vrad` and `zh_3km` are only valid for this method. The default `method="radarx"` keeps its numbers, and its docstring now lists what it takes from the two papers and what is its own. The method of Gourley et al. (2007) is not implemented: the paper (read in full) bases its three-class classifier on Gaussian-kernel density estimates of C-band data of one radar that it shows as plots, not as numbers, and its range correction and pulse-to-pulse variability cannot be reproduced from the text; the docstring says so.
