- **DOC:** The DSD scattering tables record their assumptions: `scattering_table` and the products of `dsd`, `radar_from_dsd` and `dsd_bayesian` carry `canting_std` (7 degrees) and `frequency_ghz` (2.8, 5.6, 9.4), read from the header of `dsd_scattering.csv`, and `ci/build_dsd_tables.py` takes the canting width. The sensitivity is quantified in `radarx.retrieve.dsd`: at S band 20 instead of 7 degrees of canting lowers the single-drop ZDR of a 4 mm drop by 0.65 dB and of an 8 mm drop by 1.66 dB and KDP by 26.9 %, the ZDR of gamma DSDs by 0.2-0.9 dB, and a table of 7 degrees retrieves Dm 12-21 % too low from such rain (10 degrees: 2-4 %); between 2.7 and 3.0 GHz ZDR changes by less than 0.06 dB and KDP by -4 to +9 %. The mu = -1 difference of the rain-shaft experiment of Kumjian and Ryzhkov does not come from canting or frequency. ({issue}`274`, {pull}`319`) by [@syedhamidali](https://github.com/syedhamidali)
